# National Media and Information Integrity Act — Framework Document

***Sovereign design. Domestic authority. Enforcement first.***

*Rev. 2 — supersedes the earlier draft. Reworked on two instructions: no dependence on foreign systems or international instruments, and no rule written that cannot be enforced.*

---

## 0. Design Doctrine

Four commitments govern every clause that follows:

- **0.1 Sovereign Authority:** Every rule here draws its force from domestic statute and
  domestic courts. No provision depends on a treaty, a foreign regulator, an international body,
  or reciprocity from another state. Where foreign systems are mentioned at all, they are
  mentioned as **observed outcomes to learn from or avoid** — never as authority, never as a
  model to copy. Their failures are instructive precisely because we are not obliged to repeat them.
- **0.2 Enforcement Precedes Proclamation:** No rule is enacted until its enforcement chain
  exists: who detects the breach, who investigates, who decides, what the penalty is, who collects
  it, and what happens when they refuse. A rule without a collection mechanism is not a law; it is
  a press release. **Any provision failing this test is struck from the draft, not softened.**
- **0.3 Discretion is Where Corruption Enters:** Wherever an official *may* decide, an official
  *may* be bought. Replace discretion with formulas, automatic triggers, and mandatory publication.
  The framework must work when staffed by ordinary people, not only by brave ones.
- **0.4 The Source vs. Filter Boundary:** The state may add to the information system; it may
  **never be the only path through it**. This is the boundary that separates order from capture,
  and it is not negotiable at any point in this document.

---

## 1. Why Not Import a Foreign Model

Stated plainly, because it explains the architecture:

- **The Permissive Model:** Produces exactly what we object to: trials as entertainment, crime
  as spectacle, manufactured aspiration sold to people who cannot afford it, and platforms
  answerable to no one inside our borders.
- **The Rule-Heavy Foreign Model:** Produces volumes of standards, an enormous compliance industry,
  and enforcement that stops at the border. Fines are announced, litigated for years, discounted,
  and absorbed as a cost of business. The rule exists; the behavior doesn't change.
- **International Instruments:** Advisory in practice. Signature costs nothing and compliance is
  optional, which is why they are routinely ignored without consequence. Building on them means
  building on something no one is obliged to honor.

We take the *underlying problems* seriously — they are real and they are ours — and we solve
them with instruments **we can actually execute**: our courts, our licensing power, our tax and
payment systems, and our market access.

## 2. Enforcement Architecture — Built First

Everything downstream depends on this section. Institutions before rules.

```
                       ┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
                       │       THE THREE STATUTORY BODIES       │
                       └───────────────────┬────────────────────┘
                                           │
         ┌─────────────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────┐
         ▼                                 ▼                                 ▼
┌──────────────────┐             ┌───────────────────┐             ┌──────────────────┐
│ MEDIA STANDARDS  │             │ INFORMATION       │             │ MEDIA TRIBUNAL   │
│ AUTHORITY (MSA)  │             │ INTEGRITY (III)   │             │ Fast-track Court │
│ Content Conduct; │             │ Technical Audits; │             │ Specialized Merit│
│ Fines & Licenses │             │ Forensics & Data  │             │ & Law Appeals    │
└──────────────────┘             └───────────────────┘             └──────────────────┘
```

### 2.1 The Three Bodies

| Body | Primary Function | Institutional Insulation & Appointment |
|---|---|---|
| **Media Standards Authority (MSA)** | Adjudicates content-conduct breaches; issues corrections, turnover fines, and broadcast license conditions. | Board serves staggered 6-year non-renewable terms; appointed by parliamentary supermajority from nominations by judiciary, working journalists, and public sortition; removable only by judicial process for defined statutory cause. |
| **Information Integrity Inspectorate (III)** | Investigates, monitors, gathers digital evidence, refers for prosecution; technical and forensic audit capacity. | Head appointed by parliamentary supermajority; reports annually to Parliament directly, not to any government minister. |
| **Media Tribunal** | Hears appeals from MSA and III rulings; specialist judges; fast-track statutory timelines. | Sits within the ordinary judiciary; onward appeal to higher appellate courts as of right. |

> **Non-Negotiable:** No government minister sits on, instructs, or funds any of these bodies by discretion.

---

### 2.2 Funding — The Real Independence Test

Funded by **statutory formula**: a fixed percentage (15%) of broadcast license fees plus a
statutory levy (2%) on domestic digital advertising turnover, paid directly into a ring-fenced
statutory fund. **Not an annual budget line.** A body whose budget can be reduced next year by a
displeased minister is not independent, whatever the statute claims about its board.

**Technical Minimum Staffing:** The statute mandates forensic accountants, data engineers capable
of independently auditing algorithmic ranking systems, and trained investigators — **not lawyers alone**.
A regulator that cannot technically verify an algorithmic or financial claim is merely decorative.

---

### 2.3 The Sanction Ladder — Mandatory, Published, Escalating

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    THE 7-STEP STATUTORY SANCTION LADDER                           │
├────────┬─────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Step 1 │ Advisory Note           │ Private guidance notice for minor ambiguities  │
├────────┼─────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Step 2 │ Published Finding       │ Formal public finding in permanent registry    │
├────────┼─────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Step 3 │ Equal-Prominence Fix    │ Mandatory correction: same page, slot, duration│
├────────┼─────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Step 4 │ Turnover Fine           │ Statutory fine (1%–10% of gross domestic turn.)│
├────────┼─────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Step 5 │ License Condition       │ Binding operational / monitoring conditions    │
├────────┼─────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Step 6 │ Commercial Suspension   │ Barred from processing ad & subscription rev.  │
├────────┼─────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Step 7 │ License Revocation      │ Full permanent revocation of broadcast license │
└────────┴─────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Escalation is **automatic on repeat breach within a defined 24-month window**. The adjudicator
does not choose whether to escalate — eliminating the informal negotiation point where political
or corporate pressure is applied.

---

### 2.4 Collection — The Clause That Makes It Real
- Fines are a **statutory debt to the state**, recoverable by the revenue authority using its
  existing full seizure and recovery powers.
- Unpaid fines attract statutory interest and **automatically bar the entity from state advertising,
  public contracts, and spectrum license renewal**.
- For entities without domestic physical assets: enforcement moves directly to the money layer (§5).
- **Corporate directors and local legal representatives are personally liable** for deliberate
  non-compliance and obstruction of an investigation.

---

### 2.5 Timelines with Consequences for the Regulator
- **Complaint Acknowledged:** 5 calendar days.
- **Determination Rendered:** 60 calendar days (shortened to 30 days during active elections and live proceedings).
- **Appeal Heard by Media Tribunal:** 90 calendar days.
- **Consequence:** Failure by the regulatory body to meet its statutory deadlines is reportable
  to Parliament and constitutes grounds for removal of its head for persistent failure.

## 3. Content Conduct — The Enforceable Rules

Each rule states its target and its enforcement route. Nothing here restricts reporting **on the state**.

```
                  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
                  │              THE CONDUCT DIVIDE              │
                  ├──────────────────────┬───────────────────────┤
                  │     RESTRICTED       │      FREE & OPEN      │
                  │  Courtroom Cameras,  │  Fiction & Literature,│
                  │  Crime Gore, Method  │  True Crime (Labeled),│
                  │  Detail, Undisclosed │  Investigative Press  │
                  │  Commercial Scams    │  on the Government    │
                  └──────────────────────┴───────────────────────┘
```

### 3.1 Real Crime and the Courts

| Prohibited Conduct | Domestic Enforcement Route |
|---|---|
| **Cameras & Live Broadcast in Courtrooms** | Court procedure rule; enforced via contempt of court. Written, drawn, and analytical reporting remain completely free. |
| **Crime-Scene Gore & Victim Injury Imagery** | MSA sanction ladder, entering automatically at Step 3. |
| **Naming Suspects Before Formal Charge** | MSA sanction ladder + statutory civil damages to the named person (*"Andreas B."* standard). |
| **Naming Victims, Minors, or Witnesses Without Consent** | MSA sanction ladder + statutory publication ban. |
| **Prejudicial Public Commentary on Live Proceedings** | Contempt of court applied directly by the presiding judge (*sub judice*). |
| **Paid Access to Victims, Families, or Witnesses in Live Cases** | Criminal offense for both payer and recipient. |

> **Rationale:** A trial is a solemn state proceeding to determine truth, not an entertainment
> program. Where courts have been opened to broadcast, the observed outcome has been theatrical
> performance by all parties. That is an observed failure we decline to reproduce.

---

### 3.2 Instructional Content — The Narrow, Evidence-Backed Core
- **Prohibited:** Operational method detail for suicide, weapons manufacture, explosives, fraud
  schemes, and system intrusion, where published without professional-context justification.
- **Statutory Carve-Outs Written Into Law:** Accredited cybersecurity research, criminology,
  medical/academic peer-reviewed publication, and official court records.
- **The Line is Method, Not Fact:** That a crime occurred is always reportable. *How it was executed,
  in replicable instructional detail,* is not. This distinction is enforceable in court through
  expert witness testimony.

---

### 3.3 Perpetrator Treatment
- Strict ban on publishing mass-casualty manifestos, glorification framing, and name-and-image
  saturation for mass attackers.
- Report the act, the victims, the systemic failures, and the judicial outcome.
- **Enforcement:** MSA sanction ladder, entering automatically at Step 3 with rapid escalation.

---

### 3.4 Fiction — Deliberately Left Free
- **Fiction depicting wrongdoing is not restricted.** Voluntary age classification only.
- Depiction with consequence is humanity's primary tool of moral instruction. Stripping the
  depiction of evil renders moral caution impossible. Property crime is driven by material
  deprivation, substance addiction, and low enforcement certainty — not by fiction.
- **True Crime Dramatization:** Dramatized true crime and reenactments carry a **mandatory in-frame
  watermark label** (*"Dramatization — Not Documentary Record"*). The harm is the audience mistaking
  drama for record; we regulate the labeling, not the story.

---

### 3.5 Aspiration and Commercial Deception — The Underweighted Harm
In an unequal society, manufactured aspirational deception inflicts severe, measurable damage.

| Deceptive Practice | Statutory Enforcement Mechanism |
|---|---|
| **Undisclosed Paid Promotions** | Must be disclosed in-frame, unskippable, in the first 3 seconds. Fined per violation; platform held jointly liable if monetized locally. |
| **Financial Promotion (Crypto, Forex, Gambling, "Trading Academies")** | Permitted only by licensed financial practitioners. Violation is a criminal offense with mandatory asset forfeiture of proceeds. |
| **Commercial Image Retouching & Filtering** | Mandatory visual label indicating digital modification on commercial advertising. |
| **Credit & Gambling Ads Targeting Minors** | Strictly prohibited during youth programming or on accounts under 18; enforced as broadcast/platform license condition. |
| **Credit & Debt Affordability Warnings** | Mandatory standardized loss and debt warnings on all credit/BNPL promotions. |

---

### 3.6 Incitement — Highest Stakes, Tightest Test
A single statutory test, applied exclusively by the ordinary courts:
$$\text{Incitement} = \text{Intent to Incite Violence} \land \text{Real Likelihood of Resulting Harm}$$
Both limbs are strictly required, evaluated on context, speaker's authority, reach, and content.
Offensiveness alone is never sufficient. This high threshold prevents incitement statutes from
being weaponized against government critics.

---

### 3.7 Protection of Children & Non-Consensual Intimate Imagery (NCII)
- **Monetized Child Influencers:** Earnings must be paid into a statutory protected trust;
  working-hour limits enforced; full takedown rights vest in the child upon reaching age 18.
- **Non-Consensual Intimate Imagery (NCII):** Criminal offense; **mandatory 24-hour takedown duty**
  on any hosting service with domestic users, backed by compounding per-day statutory fines.

## 4. Platforms — Regulate the Ranking, Not the Post

The decisive legal move that protects free expression while curbing systemic harm:

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    THE EDITORIAL AMPLIFICATION DOCTRINE                           │
├───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│    "Hosting stays immune. Amplification does not."                                │
├───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • Storing what a user posted is passive hosting (immune).                         │
│ • Algorithmic ranking and distribution into millions of feeds is an editorial     │
│   act, and editorial acts carry statutory responsibility.                         │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Eight Statutory Platform Duties (Above Domestic User Threshold)
1. **Annual Independent Ranking Audit:** Verified by III-accredited data engineers with full code
   access under strict confidentiality. Public summary mandatory.
2. **Published Systemic Risk Assessment:** Platforms must identify and mitigate systemic harms;
   liability attaches to *failure to assess and mitigate*, never to individual user posts.
3. **Non-Personalized Feed Default Option:** Chronological, non-algorithmic feed available via a
   single tap with zero dark patterns.
4. **Zero Engagement Optimization for Under-16 Accounts:** Algorithmic dopamine-loop recommendation
   is strictly prohibited on minor accounts.
5. **Public Searchable Political Ad Register:** All advertisements, sponsors, expenditures, and
   micro-targeting criteria retained for 5 years in an open public database.
6. **Independent Researcher Data Access:** Public reach and interaction data accessible to domestic
   academic institutions to test harm hypotheses empirically.
7. **Virality Friction Controls:** Content-neutral forwarding limits, group size caps, and new-account
   rate limits to prevent automated bot storming.
8. **Provenance Credential Display:** Mandatory support for verifiable C2PA origin metadata.

### The Stated Policy on End-to-End Encryption
> **No key escrow. No client-side scanning. No backdoors.**
> Backdoors break cryptographic security for every citizen, business, and public institution,
> and are inevitably abused. Closed encrypted communications are governed strictly at the
> metadata layer (forwarding caps, rate limits) and addressed through media literacy.

## 5. Enforcing Against Foreign Operators — Money, Not Packets

Foreign technology platforms will ignore rules that lack enforceable domestic costs. The state
attaches costs where foreign operators are genuinely reachable: **domestic revenue and market access.**

```

│ 2. TAX-DEDUCTIBILITY DISALLOWANCE: Domestic businesses cannot deduct ad spend     │

```

## 6. Authenticity — Provenance Over Detection

Detecting synthetic media through AI classifiers is a permanent, losing arms race. Establishing
origin and chain-of-custody is an engineering problem we can solve.

- **Content Credentials Standard:** Mandatory support for cryptographic provenance metadata on
  capture devices and editing tools sold domestically.
- **Mandatory Synthetic Labeling:** AI-generated media in political, electoral, and commercial
  contexts must carry indelible visual/audio labels.
- **Criminal Penalties:** Deceptive synthetic impersonation of real individuals for fraud,
  defamation, or non-consensual imagery is a major criminal offense.
- **Electoral Protection:** Unlabeled synthetic depiction of any public candidate or official
  during an active election cycle carries immediate criminal liability.
- **The Epistemic Shift:** The legal burden moves from *"prove this is fake"* to **"prove this is authentic."**

## 8. The Consequence Layer: Linking Detection to Action

Investigative journalism is the society's **detection** system. Detection without consequence
produces civic cynicism. The consequence layer binds reporting to automatic legal triggers:

- **Public Asset & Interest Declarations:** Officials and immediate families publish machine-readable
  asset disclosures; discrepancies trigger **automatic lifestyle audits by the RAB (§22)** without
  requiring discretionary official bravery.
- **Open Contracting Data:** Full public procurement lifecycles published in structured data with
  an unmaskable **beneficial ownership register**.
- **Unexplained Wealth Orders:** Judicial orders requiring officials to prove lawful source of
  assets exceeding declared income.
- **3-Year Revolving-Door Cooldown:** Regulators barred from employment with regulated entities for 3 years.

## 9. Safeguards Against This Framework Itself

To prevent future governments from weaponizing these regulatory tools against the public:

1. **Public-Interest Defense Checklist:** Available against all content claims (contribution to public debate, public status of subject, verification effort).
2. **Appeal as of Right to Ordinary Courts:** Full de novo judicial review of all MSA rulings.
3. **Mandatory 5-Year Sunset:** Every content-restricting clause expires in 5 years unless affirmatively re-enacted by Parliament following public review.
4. **Pre-Defined 30-Day Emergency Derogations:** Narrow, judicially challengeable, and **strictly prohibited from suspending investigative reporting on the state**.
5. **Functional Definition of Journalism:** Protection applies to anyone performing acts of journalism; **mandatory licensing of journalists is unconstitutional**.
6. **Advisory Rulings with Safe Harbor:** Publishers can seek prior guidance from the MSA; compliance provides complete immunity, eliminating chilling effects.
7. **Mandatory Annual Sanctions Register:** Full public transparency of every complaint, ruling, fine, and appeal.

## 10. Domestic Evidence Base — Ours, Not Borrowed

- **Pre-Enactment Baseline Research:** Commission comprehensive domestic studies on media
  consumption, indigenous language reach, institutional trust, and economic inequality.
- **Pre-Registered Outcome Metrics:** Every regulatory measure must define falsifiable success
  metrics before enactment; failure to move metrics triggers sunset expiration.
- **Independent Media Observatory:** Continuous academic monitoring of media concentration,
  ad revenues, and algorithmic reach.

## 13. The Five Generating Principles

1. **Regulate process, money, and provenance — never content itself.** Audits, disclosure, formulas, labels. The censor's desk never opens.
2. **Remove discretion at every point corruption could enter.** Formulas, automatic triggers, default publication.
3. **Make amplification accountable; keep hosting free.** Ranking is the editorial act.
4. **Discipline the state before the press.** Otherwise the framework becomes the capture.
5. **The state may add to the information system; it may never be the only path through it.**

---

### Declared System Limits
- **Encrypted Channels:** Closed end-to-end encryption cannot be backdoored without destroying
  security for everyone. It is governed only at the metadata layer (forwarding caps).
- **Foreign Reach:** Money-layer enforcement creates immense leverage but cannot stop offshore
  volunteer hosting.
- **Institutional Prerequisite:** The framework requires §2 formula funding and §7 state
  discipline to hold. If the state breaches §7, the entire system must be defended by the judiciary
  and citizen sortition juries.
