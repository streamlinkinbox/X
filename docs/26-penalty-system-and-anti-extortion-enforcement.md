### 1.1 Why Community Labor Eliminates the Bribe
- **Rich Offenders Cannot Buy Out:** Wealthy individuals cannot write a check to avoid consequences.
  They must report on Saturday morning to clean storm drainage trenches and sewers alongside everyone else.
- **Officers Cannot Monetize Labor:** An officer cannot extort labor hours for private pocket money.
  There is literally nothing for the corrupt officer to steal or pocket at the roadside.
- **Direct Public Benefit:** Instead of funding administrative bureaucracy, penalty hours maintain
  vital infrastructure: clearing irrigation canals, clearing firebreaks (§23), and patching road networks.

## Part II: Restitution Accounting — 100% to Victims, 0% to Police

When an offense causes tangible property damage or financial loss (e.g., crashing into a storefront,
stealing grain, damaging tools):

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    THE VICTIM-FIRST RESTITUTION WATERFALL                         │
├───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. 100% DIRECT TO VICTIM: All assessed restitution (calculated at 2.0x value to   │
│    cover replacement plus disruption) is paid directly to the harmed victim.      │
│ 2. ZERO POLICE BUDGET BENEFIT: Exactly 0% of restitution or labor value flows to  │
│    the police station, municipality, or citing officer.                           │
│ 3. ZERO SLUSH FUNDS: No municipal fine-revenue accounts exist. Enforcement is a   │
│    pure cost-center funded by the general budget, not an extractive business.     │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

By decoupling police station funding from citation volumes, **the predatory incentive to set speed
traps and harass citizens for revenue is completely eliminated**.

## Part IV: The 7-Day Sortition Citizen Jury Dispute Tribunal

To ensure that rogue officers cannot abuse the system through false citations, every citation can
be challenged through a fast, democratic dispute mechanism:

```
                  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
                  │          SORTITION CITATION TRIBUNAL         │
                  ├──────────────────────────────────────────────┤
                  │ 1. Citizen lodges 1-tap appeal within 7 days │
                  │ 2. Case heard by 3 randomly chosen citizens  │
                  │ 3. Evidence reviewed: dashcam vs. statement  │
                  │ 4. 2-of-3 votes determines outcome           │
                  └──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **The Missing Evidence Rule:** If the citing officer fails to provide verified dashcam or
  bodycam video evidence, the citation is **instantly dismissed with prejudice**, and the officer
  is assessed an integrity demerit point.
- **True Peer Review:** Disputes are judged by ordinary citizens serving 1-day sortition jury
  duty (§20), not by biased police commanders or career magistrates.
