***A critical policy and economic proposal inspired by the RCU design***

---

## Purpose

This document examines the proposal to abolish:
- Medical-aid subscription models;
- Commercial private insurance;
- Commercial legal-aid subscriptions;
- Speculative private pension funds based on compulsory contributions;
- Compound-interest commercial loans and mortgages;
- Sovereign bond markets and structural public indebtedness;
- Other recurring financial subscription systems that extract wealth indefinitely.

The objective is not merely to rename these financial instruments. The objective is to
replace them with institutional systems based on **real physical production, shared mutual
risk-pooling, transparent accounting, and direct access to essential goods and services**.

The RCU design provides the foundational architecture: currency and credit must represent
real commodities, warehouse claims, productive labor, and measurable capacity rather than
unlimited, compounding claims on future human survival.

## 2. Why Abolishing Subscriptions Is Necessary

Subscriptions are not automatically malicious in luxury entertainment, but **essential human
services become dangerous extortion engines when access depends on perpetual monthly payments**.

### 2.1 The Mathematical Fraud of Medical Aid & Health Insurance
Commercial medical schemes operate on an inverted profit incentive:
$$\text{Profit} = \text{Premiums Collected} - \text{Claims Approved} - \text{Overhead}$$
- To maximize profit, the company **must minimize claims paid**.
- Every approved surgery reduces company returns; every denied claim, "pre-existing condition"
  exclusion, co-payment requirement, or deductible increases executive bonuses.
- A patient does not choose when to suffer trauma, illness, or genetic disease. Treating life-saving
  medical care like a monthly streaming subscription is morally bankrupt and economically extractive.

**The Sovereign Replacement:**
- Healthcare funded through direct public provisioning and general guild taxation (§07, §15).
- Public clinics staffed by medical apprentices and masters (§14).
- 90-day physical buffers of essential Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs) and surgical
  supplies stockpiled locally (§16, §17).
- Access to care is determined by medical necessity, not whether a debit order cleared.

### 2.2 Commercial Legal Aid Subscriptions
Legal protection is an essential public right. When justice depends on private retainers or
monthly insurance fees, law becomes formally equal but practically unequal:
- **The Sovereign Replacement:** Publicly funded legal defense, independent community legal
  clinics, and assigned sortition advocates (§06, §20, §26).
- Zero hourly billing traps, zero commercial retainer fees.

## 6. Conventional Loans vs. Zero-Interest Resource Advances

### 6.1 The Destructive Nature of Compound-Interest Debt
Conventional loans enforce compounding interest, predatory collateral forfeiture, and lifelong
debt bondage. A 30-year housing mortgage forces a family to pay 2.5x to 3.0x the actual value of
their home, while auto loans trap workers in endless monthly cycles.

### 6.2 The Need for Advance Financing
A society without advance financing protects established wealth and destroys social mobility.
A young farmer needs seed and tractor access before harvest; a family needs building materials
before their house is constructed; an artisan needs tools before opening a workshop.

### 6.3 The Sovereign Solution: Zero-Interest Non-Compounding Advances


## 7. Replacements for Conventional Financing

1. **Resource Advances:** Cooperatives advance physical seed, tools, machinery hours, and
   building timber, repaid in output at harvest or project completion.
2. **Revenue-Sharing Finance:** Project capital is provided in exchange for a fixed share of
   actual output. If the venture suffers honest failure, the financier shares the loss.
3. **Cooperative Equity Ownership:** Productive workshops and factories are owned by the workers,
   with expansion funded through retained reserves rather than commercial debt.
4. **Public Grants for Essential Infrastructure:** Projects providing clean water, sanitation,
   fever clinics, and flood defenses are funded via direct civic allocations, not loans.
