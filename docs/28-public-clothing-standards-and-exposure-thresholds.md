### 1.1 No Touching, No Detention, No Arrest
- **Absolute Physical Prohibition:** It is a statutory violation for an officer to touch,
  grab, push, detain, or arrest any person for a clothing threshold infraction.
- **Non-Custodial Nature:** A person can **never go to jail** for what they are wearing.

---

### 1.2 The Remedy Precedes the Penalty
- **First Contact is an Offer:** When an exposure threshold violation is observed, the officer
  or venue operator approaches and immediately provides a clean, free covering wrap or garment
  on the spot.
- **Immediate Resolution:** If the person puts on the garment, **the matter is concluded immediately**.
  No name is taken, no citation is written, and no regulatory record is created.
- **Empirical Reality:** More than **90% of public clothing non-compliance** occurs through
  circumstance, weather changes, wardrobe malfunction, or simple carelessness — not deliberate
  defiance. Providing a free garment resolves the overwhelming majority of encounters with zero conflict.

---

### 1.3 Refusal as a Civil Infraction (Not a Crime)
- If an individual willfully refuses the offered garment and insists on remaining in the shared
  space, the officer issues a standard **civil infraction notice** (identical to a civil parking notice).
- The notice is contested or settled through the civil administration system. It creates **zero
  criminal record** and carries **zero custodial penalties**.

---

### 1.4 Formulaic Civil Escalation Without Extortion
- Escalation is strictly formulaic (1st notice: 20 RCU; 2nd repeat within 12m: 50 RCU; 3rd repeat:
  100 RCU or 4 hours community labor §26).
- The officer has **zero power to raise, lower, or settle the notice on the scene**.
- Non-payment routes to standard civil debt recovery or restorative civic maintenance (§26) —
  **never to imprisonment**. Hardship waivers are granted automatically upon verified low-income criteria.

### 4.1 Tier 1: Full Uniform as an Accountability Device
- **Identification Precedes Authority:** A uniform is an accountability device before it is a
  dress code. It carries a prominent service ID number, rank, and branch.
- **Traceability of Power:** Any citizen subject to state authority must be able to identify the
  exact officer at a glance. Plainclothes authority is strictly prohibited in routine civic
  policing — the officer who cannot be identified is the officer who cannot be held accountable
  or disqualified (§20, §25).
- **Criminal Ban on ID Obscuration:** Removing, taping over, or obscuring a badge or service number
  is an automatic criminal offense and triggers immediate suspension.

### 4.2 Tier 2: Standardized Dress Code for Administrative Staff
- Ministry officials, analysts, and desk clerks follow a published, sex-neutral dress standard
  reflecting Zone 1 modesty standards.
- **No Costumes for Desk Work:** Forcing uniforms on administrative staff buys zero public
  accountability while destroying workplace morale and wasting municipal budgets.

### 4.3 Tier 3: Senior Officials and Deliberate Visual Restraint
- Putting senior leadership in elaborate uniforms, ceremonial gold braid, and medals creates a
  **visual aristocracy** signaling caste superiority over citizens.
- **The Sovereign Rule:** The most senior person in the room must dress most simply. High officials
  wear clean, plain civilian service attire, visually reinforcing that authority in this system
  is temporary stewardship, not hereditary aristocracy.
